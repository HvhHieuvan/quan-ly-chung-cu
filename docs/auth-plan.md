# Ke Hoach Dang Nhap Va Phan Quyen

## 1. Muc tieu
Xay dung chuc nang dang nhap cho he thong quan ly chung cu va phan quyen theo vai tro nguoi dung.

## 2. Vai tro
- ADMIN
- MANAGEMENT
- RESIDENT

## 3. Thong tin dang nhap
Nguoi dung dang nhap bang:
- username
- password

## 4. Bang du lieu su dung
Bang users gom:
- id
- username
- password
- full_name
- email
- phone
- role
- status
- created_at

## 5. Xu ly du kien
- Nguoi dung nhap username va password
- He thong kiem tra tai khoan trong bang users
- Neu dung, he thong cho phep dang nhap
- Neu sai, he thong thong bao loi
- Sau khi dang nhap, moi vai tro se duoc chuyen den man hinh phu hop

## 6. Phan quyen du kien
- ADMIN: quan ly tai khoan, loai phi, toa nha, dashboard
- MANAGEMENT: quan ly cu dan, can ho, hoa don, thong bao, phan anh
- RESIDENT: xem can ho, xem hoa don, gui phan anh, dang ky the xe, xem thong bao
